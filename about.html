I started my career at the intersection of both disciplines. Having delivered over 50 client web platforms across the United States, Europe, the United Kingdom, and Pakistan, my philosophy is simple: a website is an investment in revenue, not an expensive digital brochure.

Leadership & Technical Track Record

Beyond traditional search, I work with Turing as an AI LLM Engineering Specialist, evaluating and fine-tuning advanced reasoning, coding, and search retrieval models.

This hands-on model training gives me deep, practical insight into how generative engines (Google AI Overviews, Perplexity AI, Gemini, and SearchGPT) crawl, parse, and cite authoritative sources. When I engineer a client's website, it is architected from day one to be the preferred cited source in AI answer engines.
