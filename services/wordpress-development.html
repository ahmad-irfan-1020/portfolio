GOT QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working with a dedicated WordPress engineer.

Why custom WordPress instead of a commercial theme or page builder? +

Commercial themes and page builders like Elementor or Divi try to cater to everyone, meaning they load hundreds of unused scripts, unoptimized CSS files, and thousands of nested DOM elements. This leads to slow mobile loading, low PageSpeed scores, and frequent plugin conflicts. A custom WordPress build contains only the exact code your site needs—resulting in sub-second speeds, zero bloat, and complete layout reliability.

Will my non-technical team be able to edit and publish content easily? +

Yes, absolutely. Using Advanced Custom Fields (ACF Pro) and native WordPress blocks, I build a custom, visual editing interface tailored specifically to your content. Your team can easily update headlines, swap images, create new landing pages, and reorder sections without touching a single line of code or risking broken styling.

What does your 100% Core Web Vitals Pass Guarantee mean? +

Before final project delivery and signoff, your website is thoroughly audited on Google PageSpeed Insights. I guarantee passing scores across all three Core Web Vitals metrics: Largest Contentful Paint (LCP < 2.5s), Interaction to Next Paint (INP < 200ms), and Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS < 0.1). If any metric does not pass, I tune it at zero additional cost until it does.

How does the 30-day post-launch warranty work? +

For 30 full days after your site goes live, I provide complimentary post-launch support. This includes immediate bug fixes, Search Console crawl monitoring, speed checks, and technical assistance. You won't be left hanging after launch.

Can you migrate our existing content, blog posts, and SEO rankings? +

Yes. I implement full automated database migrations preserving all your blog posts, product catalogs, customer accounts, order history, and media libraries. In addition, I perform strict 1-to-1 301 redirect mapping to ensure your established Google rankings and backlinks remain 100% protected.
