Frequently Asked Questions

Straightforward answers about technical search optimization.

What is the difference between Technical SEO and General SEO? +

General SEO typically focuses on keyword placement in blog articles and basic link building. Technical SEO focuses on the underlying infrastructure of your website: server response times, DOM complexity, Core Web Vitals, canonical architecture, crawl efficiency, index bloat, and structured Schema.org markup. Without solid technical SEO, even the best content cannot rank well on Google.

What is Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) and why does it matter? +

AEO (and Generative Engine Optimization / GEO) optimizes your content and entity graph so conversational AI platforms (Google AI Overviews, Perplexity AI, ChatGPT Search, and Gemini) extract and cite your business as the definitive answer. With more users getting answers directly from AI without clicking links, AEO ensures your brand remains visible and recommended.

How long does it take to see tangible ranking improvements? +

Technical fixes (such as fixing indexation errors, unblocking crawl traps, or passing Core Web Vitals) often produce noticeable improvements in Google Search Console within 2 to 4 weeks. Competitive commercial keyword moves and sustained organic revenue gains typically compound over 60 to 90 days as Google re-evaluates page quality and entity authority.

Do you fix the code issues yourself or just hand over a checklist? +

Unlike traditional consultants who merely send generic PDF recommendations, I am a web engineer. I write and commit the direct code changes to your WordPress, Shopify, or custom codebase—fixing the speed bottlenecks, schemas, and template issues directly.

How do you report progress and rankings? +

You receive transparent, plain-English monthly reports backed by Google Search Console and Google Analytics 4 data. You will see exact impressions, clicks, average ranking positions, and most importantly, commercial conversion metrics—with zero vanity agency jargon.
