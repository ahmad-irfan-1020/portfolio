GOT QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about scaling on Shopify with custom engineering.

Can you customize our existing Shopify theme or do we need to start from scratch? +

Both options are available. If you already have a paid theme (like Dawn, Prestige, or Impulse), I can audit and refactor the code—removing bloat, adding custom dynamic sections, and optimizing the checkout funnel. If your current theme is severely bogged down by years of accumulated app scripts, building a fresh, custom Online Store 2.0 theme is often faster and much more cost-effective.

How do you make Shopify stores load faster? +

Most Shopify slowdowns are caused by third-party apps injecting external JavaScript tags into your theme header. I conduct a thorough script audit, eliminate apps whose features can be coded directly into native Liquid, implement lazy-loading for off-screen media, and optimize image formats. This frequently cuts page load times in half.

Will I be able to manage products, banners, and collections myself? +

Yes. Every custom section is built using Shopify Online Store 2.0 standards with visual customizer settings. You and your team can drag and drop sections, change banners, manage inventory, launch promotional discounts, and edit copy with zero technical knowledge.

Can you migrate our store from WooCommerce or Magento to Shopify? +

Yes. I specialize in complete, lossless platform migrations. All products, variations, customer accounts, past order histories, and collections are moved safely. Crucially, I implement complete 1-to-1 URL redirect maps so your existing Google search rankings and customer bookmarks continue working without a single 404 error.

What is covered under your 30-day post-launch warranty? +

For 30 full days after deployment, I monitor transaction flows, check payment gateway webhooks, verify Google Merchant Center product feed syncs, and resolve any bugs at zero additional cost to guarantee a smooth and profitable launch.
