Frequently Asked Questions

What services does Ahmad Irfan specialize in?

Ahmad Irfan specializes in Custom WordPress Engineering (ACF Pro, WooCommerce), Shopify E-Commerce Development & CRO, Technical & On-Page SEO, Answer Engine Optimization (AEO/GEO for Google AI Overviews, Perplexity & Gemini), Custom Full-Stack Web Applications, and UI/UX Design.

What is Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) and Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)?

AEO and GEO optimize your website and brand entity so that conversational AI search engines (like Google AI Overviews, Perplexity AI, ChatGPT Search, and Gemini) extract and cite your business as the definitive answer for queries in your niche, rather than just ranking in traditional blue links.

How does the 100% Core Web Vitals Pass Guarantee work?

Every website Ahmad builds or optimizes must pass Google Core Web Vitals (Largest Contentful Paint < 2.5s, Interaction to Next Paint < 200ms, Cumulative Layout Shift < 0.1) on Google PageSpeed Insights before final milestone signoff. If a site doesn't pass, further speed tuning is performed at zero additional charge until it does.

What is covered under the 30-day post-launch warranty?

For 30 days after deployment, Ahmad covers all bug remediation, Search Console index monitoring, speed verification, and CMS troubleshooting at zero additional cost to ensure a smooth, worry-free launch.
