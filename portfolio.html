The Challenge & Problem

Cell City Mobiles stocks hundreds of smartphone models, tablets, and smartwatches. Each device has multiple variations (color, storage capacity, PTA approved status, warranty coverage). The Challenge & Problem

Heavy steel wire ropes are notoriously difficult to photograph for commercial catalogs and specification brochures. Physical samples are bulky, oily, and cross-sectional cutaways reveal jagged edges when cut physically. Photographing internal polypropylene fiber cores and individual galvanized wires under studio lights costs thousands and fails to highlight microscopic strand engineering.

Architectural Solution & Execution

Modeled the entire cable strand system procedurally in Blender directly from 2D engineering blueprints. Developed bespoke PBR (Physically Based Rendering) shader materials replicating zinc galvanization, oiled steel sheen, and braided fiber cores. Rendered cross-sectional sliced views, exploded assemblies, and 4K commercial hero plates ready for both print and web.

Key Engineering Deliverables

  • Parametric helical modeling allowing rapid generation of 6x19, 6x36, and 8x19 cable lay patterns.
  • Subsurface scattering and anisotropic micro-scratch PBR metallic shaders in Blender Cycles.
  • Cross-sectional cutaway visualizations showing internal strand-to-core structural tolerances.
  • Web-optimized compressed WebP renders achieving sub-100KB file sizes for online catalogs.
  • Print-ready 300DPI 4K master visual plates for international exhibition banners.
