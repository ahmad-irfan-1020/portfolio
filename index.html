03 — AI Search (AEO & GEO Framework)

Engineering sites for Perplexity, ChatGPT & Google AI.

Traditional SEO optimizes for keyword bots. Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) and Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) optimize for large language models. Drawing from 6 months evaluating LLMs at Turing, here is my 4-pillar technical framework to get brands cited as authoritative AI sources.

PILLAR 01 / GRAPH ENTITY DISAMBIGUATION

Semantic Entity Graphs & Nested JSON-LD Triples

AI search engines build knowledge graphs from entity triples (Subject-Predicate-Object). I architect deeply nested Schema.org @graph JSON-LD linking your brand directly to verified Wikidata, Wikipedia, and LinkedIn nodes so LLMs resolve entity disambiguation with zero hallucination.

✦ Perplexity Sonar & Google Knowledge Graph verification
PILLAR 02 / RAG ZERO-SHOT EXTRACTION

Direct-Answer Micro-Summaries (40–60 Words)

Modern RAG pipelines chunk web pages into 256–512 token embeddings and score them against user queries using semantic vector similarity. I structure content hierarchies with concise, declarative 40–60 word micro-summaries directly beneath question headings for instant zero-shot snippet extraction.

✦ Preferred snippet source for SearchGPT & Google AI Overviews
PILLAR 03 / DATA HIGH VECTOR WEIGHT

Fact-Dense Comparison & Benchmark Tables

Language models heavily discount marketing adjectives and prioritize structured data matrices with exact numerical metrics, comparative specs, and quantified boundaries. I engineer semantic HTML tables that RAG systems retrieve as high-confidence citation passages for commercial intent queries.

✦ Priority citation in AI product comparisons & vendor evaluations
PILLAR 04 / CRAWL ZERO-JS INGESTION

Bot-First Crawlability & Sub-200ms Token Ingestion

AI crawlers (GPTBot, PerplexityBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended) operate on tight compute budgets and rarely execute heavy client-side JavaScript. Every site I engineer serves lightweight, semantic HTML with zero DOM bloat, ensuring 100% token ingestibility in under 200ms.

✦ Guaranteed indexation across all major AI search user-agents
