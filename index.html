01 — About

A developer who thinks like a marketer.

Most developers hand you a website and walk away. I stay for the part that actually pays: getting it found, and turning visitors into customers.

I joined SkySol Media as a web developer and SEO specialist, and nine months later I was promoted to lead its WordPress and SEO teams. That happened because I care about the whole picture, not just my slice of it.

Today I split my time three ways: leading web and SEO delivery at SkySol Media, running SEO as a contract Project Manager at Zonix Tech, and taking on freelance and contract projects directly. In between, I spent six months at Turing evaluating and training AI models for US-based clients, which changed how I think about content, search, and where AI answers are taking both.

My range is the point. Development, SEO, design, and apps all sit together here, so the strategy, the build, and the rankings connect instead of being patched together afterwards. I work freelance and contract only, and everything is explained in plain language, not jargon.
